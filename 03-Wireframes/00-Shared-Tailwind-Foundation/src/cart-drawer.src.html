NUDGE Beauty Theme: cart drawer wireframe · single responsive file

The slide-in cart that opens from the header cart icon (a native <dialog>, behaviour from the Design System's drawer.js), built with Tailwind utilities and the shared components mapped to the Design Variables. It opens over any page, so the page behind it here is only a labelled stand-in. Products, prices and the free-shipping threshold are sample content standing in for the Shopify cart. Placeholders only: no photography. Pick a cart state below; the drawer opens with it. Close the drawer (close button, Esc or the dim area) to reach these buttons again. Use Width preview for 1920, 1440, 1280, 1024, 768, 430, 390 and 320 px.

Review notes: anatomy, decisions, what stays on the cart page, Shopify (open)

Purpose

Let a shopper check, adjust and leave the cart in one or two taps without losing their place on the page. The drawer is the quick view; the full cart page stays the place for the order note, delivery estimate and express checkout.

Anatomy, top to bottom

  1. Header: "Cart" with the item count, and a 44 px close button. Stays fixed.
  2. Free-shipping progress: one line and a thin bar that fills as the cart grows. Only shown when a threshold is set.
  3. Lines: small image area, name, variant, price, quantity stepper and Remove. Removing is one tap with an Undo message, no confirmation dialog. Real low-stock notes only; a typed quantity above stock is capped with a message.
  4. One add-on: a single low-priced item the merchant picks, with an Add button. Hidden when already in the cart.
  5. Footer (fixed): subtotal, saving (when there is one), the tax and shipping note, one dominant Checkout button with a lock icon, and a quiet View cart link.
  6. Empty state: a short message, a Continue shopping button and collection links.

Decisions to confirm

  • Kept light on purpose: the order note, gift message, delivery estimate, express-checkout buttons and policy links stay on the cart page (the approved cart decisions).
  • No discount-code box (Shopify takes codes at checkout), no timer, no save for later, no installment line, no gift-wrap upsell.
  • One add-on, not a carousel: the drawer is narrow and the checkout button must stay in view.
  • The drawer opens when the header cart icon is tapped or when an item is added from a product page. After an add, the new line is shown first.

Not drawn

  • Cart-recommendation widgets, subscription or shipping-protection add-ons, loyalty points (each needs an app)

Shopify build

A cart-drawer section rendered in the header group, over the native cart object and the Cart Ajax API (/cart/change.js to change or remove, /cart/add.js for the add-on, then re-render the section with the Section Rendering API). Free-shipping threshold, the add-on product and the cart link are theme-editor settings. A native <dialog> with the shared drawer.js gives focus handling, Esc, scroll lock and focus return. No app is involved.

GlobalAnnouncement bar and header (sticky): the shared sections; the cart icon is a button that opens this drawer. The footer is the shared footer. Responsive: a full-width sheet below 600 px, a 416 px panel on the right from 600 px.
