NUDGE Beauty Theme: FAQ wireframe · single responsive file

Phase 1, page 2 of 25. A native Shopify page with grouped questions, built with Tailwind utilities and the shared components (accordion, chips, buttons) mapped to the Design Variables. The announcement bar, header and footer are the shared sections. Questions and answers are sample content with bracketed values the merchant fills in; no policy, time or price is invented. Placeholders only: no photography. Use Width preview for 1920, 1440, 1280, 1024, 768, 430, 390 and 320 px.

Review notes: purpose, sections, decisions, Shopify (open)

Purpose

Answer the questions that stop a purchase or create a support message, grouped by topic and quick to scan, with a clear way to reach a person when the answer is not here.

Sections, top to bottom

  • Title and short intro
  • Topic chips that jump to each group (Orders and delivery, Returns, Products and skin, Account and payment)
  • Question groups as native accordions, one open at a time is not forced
  • Still need help: link to the contact page

Decisions to confirm

  • All answers carry bracketed values (delivery times, return window, payment methods). They must match the real policies before launch.
  • Skin advice answers stay general and never promise a result or a medical outcome.
  • No in-page FAQ search: native store search does not index a page's accordion text, and an app is out of scope.

Not drawn

  • Chatbot, helpdesk widget, ticket status, voting on answers (all need an app)

Shopify build

Template page.faq.json with a main-page intro and a faq section. Each group is a block with a heading; each question is a nested block (question text, rich-text answer). The accordion is native <details>, so it works without JavaScript. Optional FAQPage structured data is output from the same blocks. Chips are anchor links built from the group headings. No app is involved.

GlobalAnnouncement bar, header (sticky) and footer: the shared sections. Responsive: below 1024 px a one-row header with the menu in a left drawer; footer groups are tap-to-open rows below 768 px.

Orders and delivery

How long will my order take to arrive?

Orders are packed within [processing time] and delivered in [delivery time] depending on where you live. You will see the estimate at checkout and in your shipping email.

How can I track my order?

When your order ships we email a tracking link. You can also see its status in your account under Orders.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Contact us as soon as you can with your order number. We can change or cancel an order until it is packed ([cut-off time]).

Returns

What is your returns policy?

You can return [eligible items] within [return window] of delivery. The full terms are on the returns and refunds page.

How do I start a return?

Send us your order number through the contact page and we will reply with the next steps.

When will I get my refund?

Refunds go back to your original payment method within [refund time] after we receive the item.

Products and skin

How do I choose the right product for my skin?

Each product page lists who it is for, the key ingredients and how to use it. Browse by concern in the collections, or ask us for help.

Where can I find the full ingredient list?

The full list is on every product page under Ingredients. Patch-test new products if you have sensitive skin.

How should I store my products?

Keep products closed, away from direct sun and heat. The period after opening is printed on each pack ([symbol and months]).

Account and payment

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept [payment methods enabled in the store]. All payments are processed at checkout.

Do I need an account to order?

No. You can check out as a guest. An account lets you see past orders and save your addresses.

How do I reset my password?

Use Forgot password on the sign in page and we will email you a reset link.
