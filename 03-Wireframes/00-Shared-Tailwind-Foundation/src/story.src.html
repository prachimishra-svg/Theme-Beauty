NUDGE Beauty Theme: Our story wireframe · single responsive file

Phase 1, page 4 of 25. A native Shopify page (page.story) built from theme sections, with Tailwind utilities and the shared components mapped to the Design Variables. The announcement bar, header and footer are the shared sections. All copy is sample content with bracketed values; no founder name, year, place or claim is invented. Image areas are labelled placeholders (no photography). Use Width preview for 1920, 1440, 1280, 1024, 768, 430, 390 and 320 px.

Review notes: purpose, sections, decisions, Shopify (open)

Purpose

Tell the brand's narrative as a short, readable sequence: why it began, how it grew, where it is going. About us (page 3) states what the brand believes; Our story tells how it got there, so the two pages do not repeat each other.

Sections, top to bottom

  • Title, lede and one wide image area
  • Opening note in the brand's voice (a reading column)
  • Timeline of four milestones
  • Pull quote
  • What comes next, with shop and About links

Decisions to confirm

  • Years, places, names and milestones are bracketed. Nothing is stated until the merchant supplies it.
  • The opening note is unsigned in the wireframe; a name and role are theme settings.
  • Four milestones is a guide; the section supports two to six blocks.

Not drawn

  • Video player embeds, press and review widgets, a newsletter form (needs an email app; the footer handles sign-up if the store has it)

Shopify build

Template page.story.json with sections image-banner, rich-text (opening note), timeline (a block per milestone: year, heading, text), quote and cta. All content is theme-editor settings; images use image_picker. No app is involved.

GlobalAnnouncement bar, header (sticky) and footer: the shared sections. Responsive: below 1024 px a one-row header with the menu in a left drawer; footer groups are tap-to-open rows below 768 px.
