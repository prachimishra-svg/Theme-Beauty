NUDGE Beauty Theme: About us wireframe · single responsive file

Phase 1, page 3 of 25. A native Shopify page (page.about) built from theme sections, with Tailwind utilities and the shared components mapped to the Design Variables. The announcement bar, header and footer are the shared sections. All copy is sample content with bracketed values; no claim, number, certification or founder detail is invented. Image areas are labelled placeholders (no photography). Use Width preview for 1920, 1440, 1280, 1024, 768, 430, 390 and 320 px.

Review notes: purpose, sections, decisions, Shopify (open)

Purpose

Say in one scroll who the brand is, what it stands for and why a visitor can trust it, then send them on: to the story (page 4), or to the shop. The longer narrative lives on Our Story, so About stays short and values-led.

Sections, top to bottom

  • Title, one-sentence lede and a brand image area
  • Mission statement in large type
  • Three values
  • How products are made: image area and short text with a link to the ingredient pages when they exist
  • Closing call to action: shop and read the story

Decisions to confirm

  • No statistics, awards, "clean" or "dermatologist" wording or founder names: each needs proof from the merchant. Bracketed slots mark where they go.
  • Team and press blocks are optional blocks, hidden when empty.
  • The three values are sample headings; merchant replaces them.

Not drawn

  • Video embed with third-party player, review or trust widgets, careers or booking forms (apps or external services)

Shopify build

Template page.about.json with sections: image-banner (title, lede, image picker), rich-text (mission), multicolumn (values, up to four blocks), image-with-text (how products are made) and a cta section. All text, images and links are theme-editor settings; images use image_picker with the image_url filter and responsive srcset. No app is involved.

GlobalAnnouncement bar, header (sticky) and footer: the shared sections. Responsive: below 1024 px a one-row header with the menu in a left drawer; footer groups are tap-to-open rows below 768 px.
