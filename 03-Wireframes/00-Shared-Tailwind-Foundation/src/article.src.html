NUDGE Beauty Theme: blog article wireframe (v4, standard Shopify article) · single responsive file

The native Shopify article page (article.json) in the shape every Shopify blog post has: title and details, featured image, article text, tags and share, optional comments, previous and next. Built with Tailwind utilities and the shared components mapped to the Design Variables. The announcement bar, header and footer are the shared sections. Title, text, names and dates are sample content in brackets; image areas are labelled placeholders (no photography). Use Width preview for 1920, 1440, 1280, 1024, 768, 430, 390 and 320 px.

Review notes: what changed, how it maps to Shopify, decisions (open)

Why the earlier versions did not feel like a Shopify article

  • They added things a Shopify article does not have: a split hero with a byline block, a contents list, a quote band, a shop strip, an ingredient card, a newsletter band and a related-articles grid with a feature card. Shopify's article template is plain by design: the article object and, optionally, the comment form.
  • A merchant writes an article in the rich-text editor, so the page has to look right for whatever they type: headings, paragraphs, lists, images and quotes. Anything that needs special blocks inside the text cannot be expected from the editor.

New structure (everything below is a native Shopify field)

  • Breadcrumb back to the journal
  • Details: tag, article.title, article.author, article.published_at, reading time computed in Liquid
  • Featured image: article.image
  • Text: article.content (the editor's H2, H3, paragraphs, lists, images, quotes)
  • Tags (article.tags) and one Share button (copy link or the browser's share sheet; no counters)
  • Comments: only if the blog has comments on, with the native form
  • Previous and next: blog.previous_article and blog.next_article

Decisions to confirm

  • Related articles, contents list, newsletter, shop strip and a separate author card are removed. None is part of Shopify's standard article, and each added length. They can come back as optional sections in the theme editor.
  • No separate summary line: article.excerpt exists but is used on the journal cards, not repeated on the article.
  • The title sits above the image so the topic is clear in the first screen.
  • The Comments button in the review bar shows the optional comments block; by default it is off, as it is on a store with comments disabled.

Not drawn

  • Social share counters, reactions, popular posts, shoppable video and review widgets (each needs an app)

Shopify build

Template article.json with one main-article section. Blocks in the theme editor: details (title, author, date), featured image, content, tags and share, comments (article.comments and {% form 'new_comment', article %}, shown when blog.comments_enabled?) and previous and next. Structured data (Article JSON-LD) comes from the same fields. No app is involved.

GlobalAnnouncement bar, header (sticky) and footer: the shared sections. Responsive: below 1024 px a one-row header with the menu in a left drawer; footer groups are tap-to-open rows below 768 px.
