NUDGE Beauty Theme: product detail page (PDP) wireframe · single responsive file

One product page (sample product: Tinted Moisturiser), built with Tailwind utilities and shared components mapped to the Design Variables. Resize the window or open the Width preview for 1920, 1440, 1024, 768, 390, 375 and 320 px. The announcement bar, header and footer are the shared sections. Product data, ingredients, steps and copy are sample content standing in for product metafields; nothing is a real claim. Placeholders only: no photography.

Review notes: approved features, exclusions, decisions, Shopify, reuse, checks (open)

Not in the wireframe (marked No or Removed for Beauty, or not approved)

  • Review cards, photos, filters and rating distribution (Removed: need a review app); a labelled app slot is shown instead
  • Recently viewed (Removed)
  • Wishlist, ask a question, back-in-stock (Removed: apps)
  • Separate Buy Now (express checkout is the fast route)
  • Specifications table, size guide, installment message, SKU, 3D / AR (No)
  • Cash on Delivery and authenticity badges (not in the approved list; merchant content)
  • Pincode check (needs an integration)
  • Fake urgency: countdowns, visitor counts

Decisions to confirm

  • Palette: your brief says warm ivory, champagne and beige; the current tokens (Pearl Pink, Rose Quartz, Luxury Rose, Rich Burgundy, Midnight Plum) are used, because champagne and beige were retired on 2026-11-11.
  • Headings: sentence-case Cormorant, as on the homepage and PLP (the brief says uppercase; uppercase stays for labels and buttons).
  • Sticky column: the gallery is sticky from 1024 px (the info column is taller than a 900 px screen); the sticky add to cart bar keeps the action reachable.
  • Shade: none is pre-selected. Add to bag stays enabled and asks for a shade (PDP-04).
  • Reviews: a display rating plus a reviews-app slot; no review data is invented.

Shopify build

Template product.json with a main-product section (gallery, variant picker, buy buttons, selling plan, delivery lines and details accordion as blocks and snippets over the native product object and the Cart Ajax API), followed by sections for the story band, complete your routine (native complementary recommendations), reviews slot (app block area), related products (native recommendations) and FAQ. Section order and visibility, gallery layout and trust items are theme-editor settings; ingredients, how to use and safety are product metafields. No app is involved. Tailwind utilities compile into one theme stylesheet from the same token-mapped config.

Reused from the Design System (nothing duplicated)

Breadcrumb, Button, Quantity, Accordion, Message, Price, Rating, Badge, Drawer (image viewer, with drawer.js), Grid, Container, text roles, expressed as the shared Tailwind components. Page-level additions (gallery, image swatch tile, option button, choice, sticky bar, busy button, viewer variant, composed placeholders, six icons) are listed in PDP-FEATURE-ANALYSIS.md, section 3.2.
