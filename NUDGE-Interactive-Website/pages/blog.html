NUDGE Beauty Theme: blog listing wireframe · single responsive file

Phase 1, blog listing (added by request). The native Shopify blog page (blog.json), built with Tailwind utilities and the shared components mapped to the Design Variables. The announcement bar, header and footer are the shared sections. Article titles, excerpts, tags and dates are sample content standing in for Shopify articles. Image areas are labelled placeholders (no photography). Use Width preview for 1920, 1440, 1280, 1024, 768, 430, 390 and 320 px.

Review notes: purpose, sections, decisions, Shopify (open)

Purpose

Help a visitor find an article by topic and read it, and send readers who finish an article toward products. This page is the blog index; the single article is its own wireframe (the blog detail page).

Sections, top to bottom

  • Title and short intro
  • Topic chips from the blog's tags (All, plus tags)
  • Featured article: the newest article, large
  • Article grid: image area, tag, title, excerpt, author and date
  • Pagination (Shopify paginate)
  • Empty state when a tag or blog has no articles

Decisions to confirm

  • All titles, excerpts, tags, authors and dates are sample content. Reading time is computed in Liquid from the article's word count, so it is native and matches the article page.
  • Topic chips use the blog's own tags (/blogs/journal/tagged/<tag>), so they filter by page load, not in place.
  • No email sign-up block: it needs an email app. If the store has Shopify Email forms, the footer handles it.

Not drawn

  • Blog search (native store search covers articles on the search page), like or comment counts, "popular posts" ranking (needs analytics or an app), email capture

Shopify build

Template blog.json with a main-blog section over blog.articles with {% paginate blog.articles by 9 %}; featured article, tag chips (blog.all_tags) and card layout are theme-editor settings; the article card is a snippet reused by related articles. Images use article.image with image_url and responsive srcset. No app is involved.

GlobalAnnouncement bar, header (sticky) and footer: the shared sections. Responsive: below 1024 px a one-row header with the menu in a left drawer; footer groups are tap-to-open rows below 768 px.
