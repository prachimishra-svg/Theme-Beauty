NUDGE Beauty Theme: privacy policy wireframe · single responsive file

Phase 1, page 7 of 25. The layout for the store's privacy policy, the same layout as the shipping and returns pages, built with Tailwind utilities and the shared components mapped to the Design Variables. The announcement bar, header and footer are the shared sections. All policy wording is placeholder: every term, time, price and region is a bracketed slot. No legal text is written here, and nothing is a promise until the merchant adds the real policy. Use Width preview for 1920, 1440, 1280, 1024, 768, 430, 390 and 320 px.

Review notes: purpose, sections, decisions, Shopify (open)

Purpose

Make the privacy policy easy to scan: the key facts first, then the full text with a way to jump between sections. The same layout is shared with the shipping and returns pages and reused for the terms page (page 8), each as its own file.

Sections, top to bottom

  • Title and last-updated date
  • Placeholder notice (review only, not part of the live page)
  • At a glance: who is responsible, what is collected, why, and how to ask about your data in one small table
  • On this page: section links (sticky list from 1024 px, a collapsible list below)
  • Policy sections: information collected, how it is used, cookies, sharing, choices and rights, retention, security, changes, contact
  • Questions: link to contact and FAQ

Decisions to confirm

  • Section headings are samples. Shopify's generated privacy policy is one block of rich text, so the live page can only show the headings the merchant writes.
  • The at-a-glance table is a theme-editor block (rows of topic and detail). It must match the policy text exactly or be left empty.
  • The contents list is built from the headings in the policy text.

Not drawn

  • Consent-management or privacy-request apps and third-party cookie widgets (each needs an app or external service). Shopify's own customer privacy banner and data-request tools are store settings, not theme designs.

Shopify build

The text comes from shop.privacy_policy.body (Settings, Policies) and renders inside main-policy, with typography applied to the policy container (.shopify-policy__container). Theme settings add the last-updated date and the optional at-a-glance table; the contents list is a small script over the rendered headings. A custom page.policy.json reuses the same section for pages the merchant writes by hand. No app is involved.

GlobalAnnouncement bar, header (sticky) and footer: the shared sections. Responsive: below 1024 px a one-row header with the menu in a left drawer; footer groups are tap-to-open rows below 768 px.
