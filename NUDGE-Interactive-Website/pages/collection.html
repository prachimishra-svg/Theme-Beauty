Decisions to confirm (this revision follows your brief where it differs from the workbook)

  • Second image on hover is marked No for Beauty in the workbook (touch has no hover). You asked for it, so it is built for mouse screens only (hover and fine pointer); touch screens show the first image. Setting: show second image.
  • Key ingredients and Benefits filters are not in the workbook's Beauty list (the CRO matrix names ingredient as a possible metafield group). Added at your request. Brand is Removed in the workbook (one brand per store), so it is not drawn.
  • Limited edition badge is not in the workbook's badge list (sale, new, best seller, sold out). Shown on one sample card; a merchant tag.
  • Sticky filters. Not used: Sticky Filters while scrolling is Removed (deferred) in the revised workbook, so the sidebar and the sort toolbar scroll with the page.
  • Quick add vs Quick view. Followed the workbook: Quick view with a shade chooser and Add to bag, not a one-tap add.
  • Pagination stays numbered (Load more and infinite scroll are Removed in the workbook; the Design System's phone Load more is not used).

Shopify build

Template collection.json with a main-collection section over the native collection object and Search & Discovery filters; the banner, sub-collection links, filters, toolbar, product grid, promo tile, pagination and SEO description are blocks and snippets (product-card, facet-group, quick-view); the promo tile, filter groups and second-image setting are theme-editor settings. No app is involved. Tailwind utilities compile into one theme stylesheet from the same token-mapped config.

Reused from the shared components (nothing duplicated)

Breadcrumb, Chip (toggle, removable and as a link), Button, Select, Drawer (mobile filters and quick view, with drawer.js), Accordion, Field, Badge, Price, Rating, Pagination, Message, text roles. Colours, spacing, type, radius, shadow, motion and z-index come from variables.css through the Tailwind theme. Page-level additions: placeholder gradients, checkbox row, colour swatch, product-card hover behaviour.
