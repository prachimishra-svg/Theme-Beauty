01 · Color

Rose, burgundy, pearl and plum.

Rich Burgundy is the CTA and active-state colour. Luxury Rose marks recognisable brand moments (eyebrows, ratings, heading rules, large serif accents). Blush Rose is a decorative accent. Pearl Pink, white and Rose Quartz carry most of the page; Midnight Plum carries the type and the dark bands. Components use semantic tokens; only the primitive layer holds raw hex values.

Contrast — measured, not assumed

WCAG 2.x relative-luminance ratios computed from the actual hex values of the canonical tokens (2026-11-11, after the Luxury Rose and Rich Burgundy palette; the live, always-current version is the Validation panel of the Design Variables dashboard). Text needs ≥ 4.5:1 (≥ 3:1 for large text); UI boundaries and focus indicators need ≥ 3:1. White-on-burgundy was calculated, not assumed. Blush Rose is decorative only on light surfaces.

02 · Typography

Cormorant & DM Sans

A refined serif for every heading, a clear sans for everything you read and tap, product names included. Sizes are whole, even px; the larger roles step up at 600 and 1024px. Why two families

Loaded: Cormorant 400–700 as one variable file (500, 600 and 700 are used) plus italic 500, and DM Sans 400–700 as one variable file. Three font files, ~98 KB total. Request only weights inside those ranges.

10 · Responsive behavior

Layouts change, not just shrink.

Mobile-first, three breakpoints. Components switch arrangement when usability demands it, rather than being scaled down.

Breakpoints

Base · 0–599

Single-column flow, 2-up product grid, full-width primary buttons, menu in a drawer, stacked forms.

Tablet · 600+

3-up product grid, two-column forms, buttons return to natural width.

Laptop · 1024+

4-up grid, inline desktop navigation, split product layout (gallery + details), content capped at 1280px.

Rules at a glance

TopicBehavior
Touch targets≥ 44 × 44px for every tappable element, with ≥ 4px between neighbours.
NavigationBelow 1024px: icon buttons + drawer menu. From 1024px: inline links. Same markup source, different arrangement.
FormsStacked on phones, two columns from 600px. Inputs always full width of their field; 16px text.
Text-heavy componentsNo fixed heights. Min-height only; content decides size. Long words wrap (overflow-wrap).
Overflowmin-width: 0 on grid/flex children, minmax(0, 1fr) columns, images max-width 100%. Tables scroll inside their own container.
